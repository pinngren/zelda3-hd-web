How do I get zelda3_assets.dat from my ROM?

For copyright reasons, game assets are extracted from your own US copy of The Legend of Zelda: A Link to the Past (zelda3.sfc). On Linux / Chromebook Linux / macOS / Windows, place zelda3.sfc in the project folder and run: 

python3 assets/restool.py --extract-from-rom

Then drop the generated zelda3_assets.dat file above — it will be cached locally in your Chromebook's storage for instant offline play.
